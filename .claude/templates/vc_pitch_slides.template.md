The skill `.claude/skills/vc_pitch.write_slides/SKILL.md` holds the deck flow
and the pitfalls of each slide

# 1. Cover / Company Purpose

- Question: what does the company do and for whom?
- Content:
  - Company name and logo
  - One-line purpose (max 15 words): customer, pain, outcome
  - Founders, round, contact

# 2. Problem

- Question: is the pain real, big, and urgent?
- Content:
  - One customer persona who feels the pain
  - The pain in dollars, hours, or error rate
  - One typical case from a real customer
  - How customers cope today (status quo)

# 3. Solution and Product

- Question: how does the company remove the pain, and does it work?
- Content:
  - Contrast: how customers cope today vs. the outcome with the company
  - The outcome for the customer, not a list of features
  - At most 3 benefits
  - Screenshot, short demo, or one simple diagram of how it works
  - One user flow from start to outcome
  - The technical edge in plain English

# 4. Traction (Past Milestones)

- Question: do real customers pay or commit?
- Content:
  - Commercial metrics first: revenue, paying customers, growth rate, retention
  - Validation signals: pilots, letters of intent, waitlist size
  - A value and a date for every metric
  - Milestones already reached (product shipped, first customer, key hire)

# 5. Market Size and Why Now

- Question: is the market big enough to return the fund, and why is it open today?
- Content:
  - Bottom-up estimate: customers x units x price
  - TAM, SAM, and SOM, each with currency, time unit, and year
  - The beachhead segment inside SAM
  - A top-down analyst figure only as a cross-check
  - 2-3 dated trends that opened the market: technology, regulation, behavior,
    cost curve

# 6. Business Model and Financials

- Question: how does the company make money, and where does the business go?
- Content:
  - Who pays, for what, how much, and how often
  - Unit economics: gross margin, CAC, LTV, payback period
  - The main cost driver in plain English
  - Revenue path for 3 years
  - The key assumptions behind each number, linked to the unit economics

# 7. Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

- Question: how does the company win its first customers and then scale?
- Content:
  - Beachhead: the first segment, its buyer, channel, and sales motion
  - 3 phases with a time window and a numeric milestone each
  - Target CAC and payback period
  - Evidence from the traction slide on which channel works

# 8. Competition and Moat

- Question: who else solves this, and why does the company keep winning?
- Content:
  - 3-5 real alternatives, including the status quo
  - Comparison on 3-4 criteria that the customer cares about
  - The durable edge: data, network effects, switching costs, distribution, IP

# 9. Team

- Question: why is this team the one to win?
- Content:
  - Each founder: name, role, one credential related to the problem
  - Key hires and advisors
  - Known gaps and the plan to fill them

# 10. The Ask

- Question: how much money, what does it buy, and what do investors get?
- Content:
  - Amount, round, and runway in months
  - Use of funds, with shares that sum to 100%
  - 2-3 milestones the round reaches
    - The last milestone justifies the next round

  - Closing line: the vision and the reasons to invest
